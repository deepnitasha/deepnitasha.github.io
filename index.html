Before we begin

Common questions

A few things to help you feel prepared and at ease.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No special preparation is needed. A quiet setting and one or two clear questions can help you get the most from your session.

Can I choose between a call and video?

Yes. All readings are online, and you can choose a voice call or video call according to your comfort.

Will the reading predict exactly what will happen?

Tarot is best used for reflection and exploring possibilities. It does not guarantee fixed outcomes, and you remain in charge of your choices.

Can tarot replace professional advice?

No. Readings are for personal insight and entertainment and are not a substitute for medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice.
